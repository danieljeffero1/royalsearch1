Video Builder & Export Studio

Create a basic slideshow-style video using your own images and captions. This is a media assembly tool, not a generator of new AI footage.

Choose images you own or are licensed to use. Select them in the order you want them to appear.

Choose images to begin. Video export requires a browser that supports canvas recording.

The export is a basic WebM video. Voiceover is played separately and is not embedded in the exported video. Your browser may ask where to save the file.

How to use this studio

  1. Create a script in Commercial Maker or a scene plan in Movie Maker.
  2. Prepare or obtain the images you want to use.
  3. Upload those images here and add matching captions.
  4. Preview, then export your video.
